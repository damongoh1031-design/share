Grand Princess® sets out in 2028 on the popular Circle Pacific 
circumnavigation of the Pacific.

Over 94 fabulous nights you’ll experience mother nature’s vibrant tapestry on idyllic Pacific Islands, 
along Alaska’s majestic coastline and in Japan and Southeast Asia. Every sunrise brings a new,
breathtaking horizon and every port unlocks a vibrant chapter of history, authentic flavours and 
wonder.
Take your front row seat to view some of the world’s most breathtaking scenery and have plenty of 
time to relax onboard. 

From iconic cities to majestic natural wonders, each stop is a curated
encounter, with once-in-a-lifetime moments you’ll never forget.
