Follow your heart – and passion for travel! The 2026 Princess® Circle Pacific is a 115-day immersive exploration of destinations 
along the world's largest ocean, visiting an incredible 60 ports (our most ever on a single cruise) in 19 countries and touching 
four continents. Coral Princess® will be your host as you visit venture to Australia & New Zealand, the South Pacific, Southeast 
Asia, Japan, Alaska and beyond. From storied landmarks to stunning landscapes, you'll experience postcard-worthy moments 
every step of the way. And with a great early booking offer, there's even more to love!
See the sights during the day and experience the nightlife after dark with Late Night (9 p.m. or later), or Overnight calls.

offers two options: Roundtrip from Los Angeles on a 115-

More time to explore: There are several late-night & 
overnight More Ashore calls, which means more time 
strolling the sights and shops of Tokyo and toasting the 
nightlife in Sydney. You’ll stay overnight in Hong Kong so 
you can wander the countless shops or head to Victoria

Halong Bay/Hanoi will allow you experience the region's 
breathtaking beauty as well as Hanoi's nightlife. 
UNESCO World Heritage Sites: With access to more 
than 45 UNESCO World Heritage Sites, the Circle Pacific 
is a history buff's and nature lover’s dream. Enjoy unique 
views as you sail by the Sydney Opera House, explore 
the Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto, and witness 
the majestic Alaskan landscapes of Glacier Bay National 
Park. There’s so much more you’ll see, including the 
Royal Exhibition Building in Melbourne and the Singapore 
Botanic Gardens.
